LTP is a meeting that allows people to share their common hobby about computer art during 3 days. Those people compete with graphics, music and visual effects programming.

You are allowed to enter compos without to be present (but not ask for price), just give your entry before
08/24/2000 12:00
to someone who is coming (you will probably find one on #demofr or #pixel irc channels)
