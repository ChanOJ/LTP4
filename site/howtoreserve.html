ORDERING EITHERWAY

If you want to pay with a bank transfert, ask us our bank account number. Be careful to verify the amount of the transfert ;).

You can also pay using a postal money order (the french word is "Mandat Postal"). Our postal address is above. WARNING, this is very slow (so, don't send it one week before the party ;)
