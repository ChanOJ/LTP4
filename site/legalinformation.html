» Legal information

Only for legal reasons ! L.T.P. is a Friendship party, not a jail.


The organizers have the right to refuse admittance to anyone, remove anyone from the party place.

Spreading illegal software and ignoring the party rules are forbidden.

You are attending the party place at your own risk. The LTP'4 organization crew cannot be held responsible for anything.

Fighting, racism, vandalism, stealing, etc. is forbidden. When you display such behaviour you risk removal from the party place.

It is illegal to use or sell soft and hard drugs at the party place.

Coffee machines, micro wave ovens and other high-power equipment are not allowed and will be refused at entrance !

Smoking is forbiden

The main hall (including the side rooms), sleepings rooms are the only indoor places that are open to party peoples.


Of course we will organize competitions. Make sure you've read these general competition rules and the part about submitting your entry.


Entries may not have been released or made publicly accessible before LTP'4.

Entries that show an obvious lack of quality, are insulting to other people, are including porno or racism, break the French laws will be disqualified.

Competitors have to be physically present at the party place in order to participate.

Organizers will make a pre-selection if there are too many entries entered for a competition.

If there are not enough entries for a competition, the competition will be canceled.

Deadlines will be published on the LTP'4 website. Competitors are allowed to submit entries after the deadlines, but the organizers can not guarantee that these entries will be included in the competitions.

Prizes will be handed out at the party place. Competitors that won a prize will loose their rights to this prize if they are not present during the prize ceremony.

The LTP'4 organization has the right to spread all entries on CD-ROM, via internet, or any other electronic or non-electronic media.
