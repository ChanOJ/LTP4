Handdrawn graphics competition

Scanned and non-handdrawn graphics will be disqualified.

Maximum resolution : 800x600x16M.

***
The last version of ACDSee will be used to display the 4/3 ratios gfx and the last version of Sea for the others. File-formats not supported by these viewers will be disqualified.
***


Handdrawn Oldschool competition

Scanned and non-handdrawn graphics will be disqualified.

***


Raytraced graphics competition

Scanned and non-raytraced graphics will be disqualified.

The minimum resolution : 640x480x24 bits.
The maximum resolution : 800x600x24 bits.

You must include Wireframe screenshots !!

Multi-channels Tracker music competition

Only .MOD, .XM, .IT, .S3M, format will be allowed.

Each song will be played for a maximum of 4 minutes.
NO IT COMPRESSION !!
Maximum size is 1.39 megabytes (1,457,664 bytes) (must fit in a floppy without being zipped or rared or anything else ;).

***
Fast Tracker will be used to play .MOD and .XM modules.
Impulse Tracker will be used to play .IT and .S3M modules.

WARNING : TRACKS will be displayed on big screen, CLEAR ALL TEXT in samples for the compo version, of course you can give us a RELEASE version with texts inside.
***

4 Channels Tracker music competition

Only .MOD tracker-based music will be allowed.

Each song will be played for a maximum of 4 minutes.
Maximum size is 512 kilobytes (524,288 bytes) !

***
Fast Tracker will be used to play .MOD

WARNING : TRACKS will be displayed on big screen, CLEAR ALL TEXT in samples for the compo version, of course you can give us a RELEASE version with texts inside.
***


Mp3 music competition

Only .MP3 music will be allowed.

Each song will be played for a maximum of 4 minutes.

***
 Winamp will be used to play .MP3 songs
***


ChipTune music competition

Only .MOD .XM .IT .SID .YM music will be allowed.

Each song will be played for a maximum of 1 minute.
Maximum size (uncompressed) is 10 kilobytes (10,240 bytes) !

***
Fast Tracker will be used to play .MOD and .XM modules.
Impulse Tracker will be used to play .IT modules.
LittleSid plugin will be used to play .SID modules.
STsound will be used to play .YM modules.

WARNING : SID and YM tunes must be given on PC DISK
 ***


Website competition

The website must be viewable with either
Microsoft Internet Explorer 4 or Netscape Navigator 4.
When you use plug-ins (flash, shockwave...), include them with your entry.
Java, Javascript, Asp, Php supported.
Website must be done ONLY for LTP !
Of course could be a java demo or flash demo.
