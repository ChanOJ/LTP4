» How to come ?

By plane

Take the plane to Paris.
You'll land on Orly or Roissy Charles de Gaulle.

Orly -> Crosne (map -> pdf, gif)

Take the OrlyVal to Antony. Take the RER B to Châtelet les Halles.

Take the RER D (D2 subline) to the Montgeron-Crosne station.
Then follow the By Feet section.

Roissy Charles de Gaulle -> Crosne (map -> pdf, gif)

Take the RER B to Gare du Nord or Châtelet les Halles.

Take the RER D (D2 subline) to the Montgeron-Crosne station.
Then follow the By Feet section.

By train

From France

Follow this link www.sncf.fr :-)

Then follow the By Feet section.

From other Countries

Take the train to Paris.

If necessary, take the Metro (U-Bahn or Subway, as you want ;) to the
next RER D station (map -> pdf, gif).

Take the RER D (D2 subline) to the Montgeron-Crosne station.
It takes about 20mn (map -> pdf, gif).

Then follow the By Feet section.

By road (car, bus, bicycle, ...)

Crosne is close to Villeneuve St Georges,
south-east close to Paris, on the right of the Aéroport d'Orly airport.
Here is a map (492 kB, 1991).
Crosne is in a green circle and the Party Place is pointed by a green cross.

Here is a bigger map (416 kB, 1987) for those who come from North or East.

Textual indications :

The best way to come from far is to join the A86 highway.
If you're coming from the North/East, you must exit the A86 at the N6 exit (just after the big Pernaud building looking like an inverted pyramid).
If you're coming from the South/West, you must exit at the Melun exit. Then the N6 will be indicated.

- once one the N6, feel free to drive a while (90 Km/h maximum). The road follows the RER railway. You'll arrive at the Villeneuve St Georges railway station (on the right. 50 Km/h maximum). Then the road goes up on a bridge, with a red/orange/green signal (dunno the english word ;). After the bridge the road turns slightly left.
- keep your left, you have to turn left at the red/orange/green signal just before the right turn.
- you cross a little bridge above a river. Take the first road on the hard right (you cannot go ahead, so no pb).
- drive quietly (50 Km/h maximum), you'll arrive to the Party Place, on your right (photo).

By feet

The way from Montgeron-Crosne railway station to the party place is VERY simple. Here is a map (18 kB, 1991). Textually, you must take on your left after the bicycle parking, walk under the bridge, walk about 500m and when you cannot continue straight ahead then take on your left, walk about 300m and you'll arrive to the party place (photo).

There is also a bus every hour between the Railway Station and the party place. The line is named E and the stop for the party place is named Boileau (say "BOA LO").
